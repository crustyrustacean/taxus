### Themes (Nope)

Taxus does not have, nor will ever have, a theming system.

Theming systems are heavy and by necessity overly opinionated. They also bring in added complexity as a common solution is to use version control. This puts you in the position of relying on someone else's work, we know what that means.

### Tailwind (Nope)

Taxus does not use legacy build systems for styles. Enough said.

### Simple SASS Compilation

Taxus takes the approach of leaving you in charge, do your own CSS using (hopefully) modern standards. The build system compiles it and outputs it with the rest of your site. Done.
